How we started

Ayush Nylon Brush was set up in 1990 in Prem Nagar, Sion West, a lane of brush makers off Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg.

The catalogue grew from what customers kept asking for. Laboratories nearby needed test tube and beaker brushes. Dairies needed long reach bottle brushes. Mills needed small machine brushes that fit a bracket. We made each one and kept making it.

How we work today

We manufacture eight brush families in nylon, polypropylene, coir and horse hair, on twisted stainless wire, aluminium, PVC and wooden backs.

What we make

Eight families, one workshop

  • Test tube brushes. Test tubes, beakers, feeding tubes and narrow glassware.
  • Bottle brushes. Baby bottles, dairy cans, beverage bottles and jars.
  • Tube and pipe brushes. Pipes, condenser tubes, drains and machine nozzles.
  • Machine brushes. Grain mills, filling lines and conveyors.
  • Coir brushes. Heavy scrubbing where nylon is too soft.
  • Cobweb dusters. Ceilings, AC vents, fans and radiators.
  • Dairy and cow brushes. Cow brushes and dairy washing brushes.
  • Custom brushes. Made to your drawing, sample or machine size.
